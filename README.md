# Intro-to-Steganography


🔎 CASE 047 — Operation NIGHTFALL

Case: 047
Operation: NIGHTFALL
Classification: Training Exercise
Focus: Digital Steganography
Objective: Identify and recover information hidden within ordinary-looking files.
🕵️ Mission Briefing

You've been given a collection of files recovered during an investigation.

At first glance, the files appear ordinary.

But in cybersecurity, ordinary doesn't always mean harmless.

Your task is to investigate the evidence and determine whether information has been concealed within the files.

You won't be given the answer.

You'll need to:

    Observe → Investigate → Discover → Reconstruct

Every piece of evidence may tell you something different.
Your mission

    Examine the provided evidence
    Identify anything unusual
    Investigate the files using appropriate tools
    Recover hidden information
    Piece together what you discover

The files are the clues. Your job is to connect them.
🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, you should be able to:

    Understand what steganography is
    Explain the difference between steganography and cryptography
    Identify common forms of digital steganography
    Inspect suspicious files for hidden information
    Use basic steganography tools
    Approach a file-based investigation methodically
